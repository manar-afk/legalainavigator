RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on this 1st day of October, 2024, by and between:

LESSOR / LANDLORD:
Mr. Rajesh Sharma, residing at #142 Palm Meadows, Whitefield, Bengaluru, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the "Lessor", which expression shall include his heirs, executors, and assigns).

AND

LESSEE / TENANT:
Ms. Ananya Rao, currently residing at #25 Indiranagar 1st Stage, Bengaluru, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the "Lessee", which expression shall include her permitted successors and assigns).

RECITALS:
WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute lawful owner of Apartment #402, Green Orchid Apartments, Koramangala 4th Block, Bengaluru (the "Demised Premises"); and
WHEREAS the Lessee has requested and the Lessor has agreed to grant a lease of the Demised Premises on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

SECTION 1: TERM AND DURATION
1.1 The lease granted hereunder shall be for an initial period of eleven (11) months commencing on October 1, 2024, and expiring on August 31, 2025, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of Section 8.
1.2 The parties may renew this Agreement upon mutually agreed terms by executing a written renewal agreement at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the term.

SECTION 2: RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 The Lessee shall pay to the Lessor a monthly rental of INR 35,000 (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand only), exclusive of society maintenance charges.
2.2 The monthly rent shall be payable in advance on or before the fifth (5th) day of each English calendar month directly into the Lessor's designated bank account.
2.3 If the rent is in arrears for more than ten (10) days, a late payment charge of INR 500 per week of delay shall apply.

SECTION 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Upon execution of this Agreement, the Lessee has deposited with the Lessor an interest-free refundable security deposit of INR 1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only).
3.2 The security deposit shall be refunded to the Lessee within fourteen (14) business days from the date the Lessee peacefully vacates the Demised Premises and hands over keys to the Lessor, subject to deductions for:
    (a) Any unpaid rent or utility bills; and
    (b) Documented actual costs of repairing any physical damage to the premises beyond normal wear and tear.

SECTION 4: LOCK-IN PERIOD
4.1 Both parties agree to a mandatory lock-in period of six (6) months starting from October 1, 2024, and ending on March 31, 2025.
4.2 During this lock-in period, neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.
4.3 In the event the Lessee vacates the premises during the lock-in period without fault of the Lessor, the Lessee shall remain liable to pay rent for the unexpired portion of the lock-in period.

SECTION 5: USE OF PREMISES
5.1 The Demised Premises shall be used exclusively for private residential dwelling purposes by the Lessee and her immediate family. Commercial activities, subletting, or sharing with paying guests are strictly prohibited without prior written permission.
5.2 The Lessee shall not cause nuisance, annoyance, or hazard to neighbours and shall abide by the registered society bylaws.

SECTION 6: MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
6.1 The Lessee shall bear the cost of minor day-to-day repairs up to INR 1,000 per repair instance (such as replacement of bulbs, washer taps, minor plumbing).
6.2 The Lessor shall remain responsible for major structural repairs, dampness, roof leakages, and external facade maintenance, provided notice is promptly given by the Lessee.

SECTION 7: RIGHT OF ENTRY AND INSPECTION
7.1 The Lessor or authorized representatives shall have the right to inspect the Demised Premises during reasonable daylight hours with at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice to the Lessee, except in emergency circumstances.

SECTION 8: TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
8.1 Termination for Convenience: After the expiration of the six-month lock-in period (i.e., from April 1, 2025 onwards), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
8.2 Notice Delivery: Any notice required under this Agreement shall be served in writing via registered post, courier, or acknowledged email to the contact addresses provided above.
8.3 Immediate Termination for Material Breach: The Lessor may terminate this Agreement and require the Lessee to vacate only if:
    (a) The Lessee fails to pay the monthly rent for two (2) consecutive months; or
    (b) The Lessee commits a material breach of Section 5 (Permitted Use);
    PROVIDED THAT the Lessor has first served a formal written cure notice specifying the breach, giving the Lessee a minimum of fourteen (14) days to cure such default, and the Lessee has failed to cure within that period.

SECTION 9: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
9.2 The civil courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any claims or disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Lessor and Lessee have signed and delivered this Agreement on the date first written above.

[Signed]
Rajesh Sharma (Lessor)

[Signed]
